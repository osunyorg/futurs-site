Reshuffle the cards to co-create an equitable and thriving society

body: >-


FUTUREs is a modular and cooperative game for citizens to become agents of change. It offers an optimistic vision based on imagination and action for players to support climate, biodiversity, social justice and wellbeing. The game combines strategy and creativity: it can be used as a board game, educational inventory and workshop support. FUTUREs' 128 cards will give you a better understanding of today's issues while inspiring you to join the happy adventure of co-creating tomorrow's world. It can be played by 2 players or more, aged 6+, with 5+ alternative game rules.

128 cards to imagine a thriving future
FUTUREs aims to empower citizens to become agents of change through several card games, an interactive card directory, as well as workshops to help citizens connect with their emotions and imagine the future.

Board game
Multiple rules to play inclusive games with easy dynamics to have fun while learning. Game modes include individual, team, competitive and cooperative rules for all players.

Card directory
Directory of the 128 cards to better understand, learn and take action for the future. Articles, podcasts, news articles, reports, art performances, and solutions are featured.

Educational resources
A set of workshops to use the cards helps people connect, get creative, and take action. Fit for educators, students, companies, policymakers, and changemakers.

Playing for change
Education for sustainable development is needed with converging crises affecting citizens in their day-to-day lives. We need a new inspiring narrative engaging people to implement the just transition. The education curriculum is often behind today’s reality and there is a greater need for inclusive, hopeful, and holistic tools to empower a rising generation of leaders. There is a great opportunity to propose alternatives giving citizens the ability to understand, be inspired, and act for a thriving future.

The Council of the Future
The Council of the Future is an invitation to imagine the world of tomorrow so that it meets the needs of future generations.  With the FUTUREs cards in hand, participants will be following in the footsteps of their great-grandchildren, adopting a forward-thinking approach. In a spirit of intergenerational justice, the workshop is an opportunity for CEOs, policymakers, and leaders to sketch out the contours of tomorrow's world. The concept is simple: a group of citizens responds to a problem by proposing a collective vision and an action plan in line with the needs of future generations. Learn more

The 128 cards

Generalist
Explore the reality of today’s world with cards representing the climate, biodiversity, social justice and wellbeing.

You will also find cards with quotes.

Hope
Explore the possibilities with a transition towards what the world could become.

These cards invite you to imagine a thriving and prosperous future.

Vigilance
Understand the risks we face in case of inaction.

These cards focus our attention on key issues, inviting us to seize opportunities to make the necessary shifts.

Problems
Identify the barriers to change preventing the just transition towards an equitable and sustainable future.

These cards map the main roots of the crises we face.

Actions
Discover the solutions to respond to the challenges of our time with individual and systemic actions.

These cards demonstrate that we collectively have the power to make change.

A transformational initiative
♻️  Committed and ethical: a   sustainable product manufactured in Europe and standing for strong ethical values. 💡 Inspiring and hopeful:  a joyful and inspiring approach to current issues focused on solutions. 🎲 Dynamic and interactive:  a tool facilitating interactions and social cohesion for the development of new skills. 🚻 Inclusive and modular: a game with simple rules that adapts to all types of audiences and contexts. 🌍 Original and empowering: a unique approach to unite, inspire and encourage action for a better world.

Our next steps with you

Print and distribution
The game is ready to be printed in English. We now need to secure funding to print and distribute it. We are committed to print it with sustainable materials.

Promotion
We are looking for opportunities to promote FUTUREs and its values to a wide range of citizens. We want the game to become accessible for all.

Partnerships
We are exploring ways to find reliable and committed partners to join forces in service to citizens. We are looking at influential education organisations.


After a successful launch in France in December 2023, we are looking for funders and partners to take FUTUREs to the next level.  The game is ready in its English version. Reach out to Niels in order to explore ways to partner!

The global initiative takes a whole-of-system approach to support countries in tackling the climate crisis by harnessing the critical role of education.
